# Additional artwork credit correction

The Mr. Turner (2014) recommendation uses the official US portrait theatrical poster created by Sony Pictures Classics, published in October 2014 by We Are Movie Geeks, replacing the landscape artwork used during initial QA.

- Announcement: https://www.wearemoviegeeks.com/2014/10/gorgeous-us-poster-for-mike-leighs-mr-turner-is-here/
- Artwork: https://www.wearemoviegeeks.com/wp-content/uploads/MR-TURNER-final-poster-560x828.jpg
- Local export: ../posters/reframed-mr-turner.webp

All 43 recommended-viewing artworks on these three lessons are reproduced complete and resampled to identical 480×720 portrait exports, without added letterbox canvases or trimming poster titles/credits. Native portrait proportions vary slightly; standardization uses resampling rather than artwork cropping. Original artwork remains copyrighted to its respective rights holders.
