01 · 楔形构图01 · THE WEDGE

想让人物被环境托住，又不显得画面呆板。Let the landscape support a character without making the image static.

怎样安排画面Staging

用背景元素组成两个楔形：一个占画面约三分之一，另一个占三分之二；一明一暗。把注意力中心放在两块三角形交界的线上。Create two wedge shapes with elements in your background. One will take up 1/3 of the frame, the other will inhabit the remaining 2/3. Select one to be light in value, the other dark. Intersect your focal point with the line between the two triangles.

02 · L 形构图02 · THE L-COMP

想用身体或场景的一角，把视线留在人物身上。Use a body or a corner of the setting to hold attention on the character.

怎样安排画面Staging

用一个或多个元素拼出大写 L，可以竖放或横放。组成 L 的各部分应有接近的明暗，并与背景拉开对比。Arrange one or more elements in your frame to create the shape of a capital “L”. This L-shape can be vertical or horizontal. Ensure all elements that comprise the L-shape contrast against the background and are of the same value as each other.

03 · 透过帘幕03 · THROUGH THE CURTAIN

想让观众像在偷看，或隔着一层东西接近人物。Make the audience feel they are peeking in or approaching through a barrier.

怎样安排画面Staging

在前景两侧摆出竖向元素，像两片帘幕；把明暗反差明显的主体放在中间空隙。空隙可以居中，也可以落在左或右三分之一处。Arrange two vertical elements in the foreground of your frame. This will act as your “curtain”. Place your focal point of contrasting value in the negative space between your curtains. The negative space between these curtains can be center frame, or place on the left or right third of frame.

04 · T 形构图04 · THE T-COMP

想把人物牢牢钉在远方的地平线上。Anchor a character firmly to the distant horizon.

怎样安排画面Staging

让背景的地平线穿过主要人物的头部或颈部。地平线可以处在任何高度，关键是与头部这个注意力中心相交。Intersect the head or neck of your primary character with the horizon line in the background of your image. The horizon line may be at any height within the composition so long as it instersects your focal point (the head of your character).

05 · 摇篮形构图05 · THE CRADLE

想让人物像被一个空间接住、包围。Let the space cradle or enclose the character.

怎样安排画面Staging

在画面下方三分之一处组成 U 形，把主要主体和其他兴趣点放在 U 形形成的怀抱里。包围感不一定温暖，也可以令人受困。Create a U-shaped element occupying the bottom 1/3 of your image. Place your focal point (and additional points of interest) within the cradle created by the U-shape element.

06 · 洞穴式遮挡06 · GROTTO

想让观众只能看见一部分，产生窥视或受困感。Restrict the view to suggest concealment, curiosity or confinement.

怎样安排画面Staging

在前景放一个大而暗的物体，遮住部分场景；把中景主体放在前景物体内凹的开口里。Create a large, dark foreground element which restricts your audiences view of the scene. Place your focal point in the middle ground of the composition within the concave of the foreground element.

07 · X 形构图07 · THE X-COMP

想让多条动线在同一处碰撞，画面更有劲。Bring lines of movement into collision for a more energetic image.

怎样安排画面Staging

用场景元素组成两条交叉斜线。主体可以围绕交点分布，不必正好压在交点上；较高机位常有利于形成 X 形。Create two intersecting diagonal lines using elements in your composition. The focal point may be grouped around, but does not have to be placed on, the intersection of the lines. High angles often help facilitate the creation of this shape.

08 · 大人物看小目标08 · BIG OBJECT LOOKING AT SMALL OBJECT

想让观众跟着一个人，望向远处另一个目标。Look with a foreground character toward a distant subject.

怎样安排画面Staging

把背对摄影机的人放在最左或最右的近景；将他注视的对象放在较远的中景，落在剩余空白的中央。Place your character in the far left or right foreground of your scene, with their back facing the camera. In the distant middle ground, position the subject your character is looking at within the center of the remaining negative space.

09 · O 形构图09 · THE O-COMP

想让一群人或一组物体，被看成一个整体。Make several people or objects read as a single unit.

怎样安排画面Staging

把元素排成圆形，圆内尽量不再放其他构图物。这样圆周上的各个部分会被视为一个整体。Arrange elements in your frame into the shape of a circle. Refrain from placing any compositional objects within the circle. By doing this, you will unify the individual elements that comprise the circle into a singular compositional element.

10 · H 形构图10 · THE H-COMP

想让两方势均力敌，同时保留它们之间的联系。Give two sides equal weight while keeping them visually linked.

怎样安排画面Staging

在前景或中景设置两个竖向元素，再让背景中的一条横线连接两者。两个竖向元素要有相等的视觉分量。Create two vertical elements in the middle- ground or foreground of your frame. Run a horizontal line in the background between the two elements. In this composition, each vertical element shares equal weight.

11 · 三颗头11 · THREE HEADS

想用三张脸，一眼交代前后层次和人物关系。Use three faces to establish depth and relationships at a glance.

怎样安排画面Staging

把三个人的头安排在画面中央，但拉开与摄影机的距离。中景头部约为前景的一半，背景头部再约为中景的一半。Arrange the heads of three characters in the center of your frame. Stagger their distance from the camera, so that the middle ground head is 1/2 the size of the foreground head, and that the background head is 1/2 the size of the middle ground head.

12 · 感叹号构图12 · THE EXCLAMATION POINT!

想用一个强烈的指向，把观众直接带到重点。Point the audience decisively toward the important detail.

在画面左或右放置一个大的倾斜元素；普通物体也可以通过透视呈现近大远小的斜面。利用它把视线引向主体。Arrange a large angled element on the right or left side of your frame. You can create this angle by placing an non-angled object in perspective, so that it diminishes in size. Use that element to direct your viewers eye to your focal point.

13 · 聚光灯构图13 · THE SPOTLIGHT

想让某个人像登台一样，成为不得不看的中心。Make a character feel like the unavoidable center of a stage.

怎样安排画面Staging

用聚光灯照亮主体周围的空气与空间。光形成的形状引导视线，同时作为一块明暗底色，让主体与背景分开。Use a spotlight to illuminate the atmosphere surrounding your focal point. This shape will lead your viewer’s eye to the focal point, while creating a value flag that provides contrast against it.

14 · 透视缩短14 · FORESHORTENING

想让手、身体或物件，像要伸出银幕。Make a hand, body or object reach toward the viewer.

怎样安排画面Staging

让主体沿三维透视朝摄影机延伸，以这段延伸把视线带入画面。广角镜头会夸大近大远小的效果。Allow your focal point to extend toward the camera in three-dimensional perspective. The extension of that element will guide the viewers eye into the frame. A wide-angle lens will exaggerate this effect.

15 · 封住画面底部15 · THE BOTTOMLESS

用一个元素占据画面下方三分之一，阻止视线从底部滑走，把注意力留给中央区域和主要主体。Create a bottom 1/3 element in your frame that blocks the audience’s eye from leaving through the bottom of the frame. This will focus their attention on the central elements of the frame, such as your focal point.

16 · 扩音器式纵深16 · THE MEGAPHONE

想让一排东西越伸越远，带观众沿斜线看进去。Lead the eye into depth along a receding diagonal row.

怎样安排画面Staging

把大小相近的元素等距排成直线，再让这条线斜着远离摄影机。透视会形成带领视线斜穿画面的形状。Arrange similarly sized elements in your frame into a straight line, equidistant from each other. Then, angle that line in perspective away from the camera. This will create a shape that leads your audience’s eye diagonally through the frame.

17 · 对峙17 · STANDOFF

想让观众夹在两个人之间，左右都不能忽略。Trap the viewer between two equally important opponents.

怎样安排画面Staging

在画面左右两边放两张大小相等的脸，中间留空。观众必须决定看哪一边，形成视觉紧张感。Arrange two equally sized faces on either side of the frame. Leave the center of the frame as negative space, so that the audience must choose which side of the frame to focus on. This will create visual tension.

18 · 长路18 · THE LONG ROAD

想让人物前方像还有很远的路，突出距离和方向。Emphasize distance and the path ahead of a character.

怎样安排画面Staging

让道路向远方延伸，把主体放在道路形成的空白中。原文建议使用较高的“2/3 angle”机位强化这块空间；这是讲义的描述，不是固定机位标准。Place your focal point within the negative space created by a road extending into the distance. You can accentuate this negative space by placing your camera at a high 2/3 angle.

23 · 山峰式群像23 · THE MOUNTAIN RANGE
