02 · 建立化学反应与关系曲线02 · Creating Chemistry & Genre

先画关系变化，再决定镜头如何连接他们。Map the relationship before designing the shots.

建立化学反应

化学反应，是两个或更多银幕人物之间可被感受到的能量与关系。它能让人物可信，增强情绪冲击，但不像图像和声音那样能被直接交付。演员间的神奇瞬间转瞬即逝；剪辑节奏脆弱，细节可能打破幻觉，人的微妙表情也很难捕捉。因此，要思考怎样保存这种联系。

Creating Chemistry

Chemistry is the perceptible, energetic relationship between two or more individuals on screen. Chemistry is a valuable asset to building authenticity and believability, increasing the emotional impact of a film. Energy isn’t a token deliverable like image or sound, so it is tricky to convey and carry through to the audience. Magical moments between actors are fleeting. Rhythm is delicate and difficult to maintain in the edit. Small details can subliminally impact audiences, breaking the illusion. The subtlety of human expression is tricky to capture. So, how do we preserve chemistry?

关系类型

类型：讲义将爱情片概括为经典爱情、悲剧爱情和爱情喜剧三类；同样的方法也可用于其他关系。

Genre

Romance films fall into 3 categories: classic, tragedy & comedy. We’ll speak in terms of romantic relationships, but the same tools can be used for any type of relationship.

经典爱情

经典爱情中，两位灵魂伴侣受外在力量阻碍，最终克服障碍走到一起。友情或家庭版本常呈现为冒险、体育励志或人物成长。

Classic Romance

Classic romance: Two soulmates are challenged by an exterior force that keep them apart, but they defeat it and end up together in the end. Films with platonic or familial soulmates often take the form of adventure, sports drama or triumphant character study.

悲剧爱情

悲剧爱情中，外力最终拆散两人；友情或家庭版本常成为惊悚、苦乐参半的人物片或家庭剧情。

Tragic Romance

Tragic romance: Two soulmates are challenged by an exterior force that ultimately tears them apart. Films with platonic or familial soulmates often take the form of thriller, bittersweet character study or family drama.

爱情喜剧

爱情喜剧中，阻碍主要来自双方的内部冲突，直到他们成长改变；友情版本就是伙伴喜剧。

Romantic Comedy

Romantic comedy: Two soulmates are kept apart by their own personal, internal conflicts that keep them at odds until they grow and change. When the two soulmates are platonic, we call it a buddy comedy. Knowing this, we can intentionally create cinematic moments that communicate connectedness or disharmony depending on the moment in the story we are in.

经典爱情的连接曲线

经典爱情的连接感有三轮明显起伏，最后达到顶峰。这是讲义的教学模型，不是所有电影必须遵守的定律。

Classic Romance: Connection Curve

A classic romance has 3 distinct peaks and valleys of connectivity, the height of their connection peaking at the end of the film.

悲剧爱情的连接曲线

悲剧爱情通常在前半段、且在第三幕前达到顶峰；深谷式起伏会产生失调和过山车感，稳步上升后骤降则把冲击集中在悲剧性分离。

Tragic Romance: Connection Curve

In a tragic romance, the height of connectivity typically peaks in the first half, and certainly before the 3rd act. Deep valleys create a dysfunctional, rollercoaster effect. A steady build with a sharp drop off will focus the impact on the tragic separation.

03 · 用画面连接或分开人物03 · Connecting and Separating Characters

不改对白，先改背景里的连线和分隔。Change visual links and divisions before changing dialogue.
化学反应的视觉技巧Chemistry Visualizing Techniques

连接两点

连接两点：利用背景元素，在两个人物之间形成连线，加强关系。

Connect the Dots

To strengthen a connection, use elements in the background of your composition to draw a line from one character to the other.

统一取框

统一取框：用同一个背景元素把两人或多人一起框住，让他们成为一组。

Unified Framing

To unify two or more characters, use a single background element to frame them together.

分隔取框

分隔取框：用背景元素切开画面，让两个人各在各的框里，表现断裂。

Divided Framing

To disconnect two characters, use background elements to divide the composition, framing them apart from each other.

© Film Reframed · Cinematic Chemistry · p. 3

04 · 视线、眼睛高度与分组04 · Eyelines, Eye Level & Grouping

他们在看同一件事吗？他们被排成一组了吗？Do they look toward the same goal and belong to the same group?

视线

视线：朝同一方向看，暗示共同目标或意图；看向不同方向，暗示意图不同、疏远或各走各路。图示比较统一与紧张。

Eyelines

Characters looking in the same direction communicate a sense of unified purpose or intention. Characters looking in different directions communicate differing intentions, creating disconnection or a sense of going seperate ways.

眼睛高度

眼睛高度：越接近同一水平，越容易被读成处于同一立场、看见彼此、心意相通。高度差异暗示不同观点，可能制造紧张或不和。

Eye Level

The more aligned the characters eye level, the more we see them on the same page. This literally conveys they see each other, are connected, and share a unified state of mind. Disperate eye levels communicates different perspectives, create a feeling of tension or disharmony.

人物分组

分组：人物靠成一个整体，会暗示联系；拆成不同组，则让观众读出他们彼此隐含或明确的关系。

Grouping

Characters together in a singe unit imply implicit connection. Separating groups show an audience each character’s subliminal or explicit relationships to one another.

© Film Reframed · Cinematic Chemistry · p. 4

05 · 重叠、距离与形状05 · Overlap, Distance & Shape

现场站得近，不等于银幕上看起来亲近。Physical closeness is not the same as closeness in the frame.

重叠

重叠：两个或更多人物在画面里重叠，观众更容易把他们当成一个整体；拉开则强调彼此间的空隙。图示对比统一与紧张。

Overlapping

Overlapping two or more characters causes your audience to take them in as a singular unit. Distancing two or more chararacters will accentuate the space between them.

实际距离与画面间距

实际距离与画面间距：前者是演员在三维空间中的物理距离，后者是二维画面中两个身影之间的距离。摄影机摆在哪，会加强或削弱实际距离的感觉；现实中离得很远的人，也能通过画面里较小的间距显得相连。

Proximity vs Spacing

Proximity is the perceivable physical distance between two actors in the 3-dimensional space. Spacing is the 2-dimensional distance between two figures in a composition. Where you place the camera will either accentuate or deemphasize the physical proximity between two characters. Characters who are physically distant can be visually connected with close compositional spacing.

形状语言

形状语言：让人物在画面上共同形成一个二维形状，可以强化亲密程度；也可利用这个形状本身的象征意义。

Shape Language

Creating a 2-dimensional shape with your characters will reinforce the closeness of their relationship. Additionally, you can employ the symbolic meaning the shapes communicate.

© Film Reframed · Cinematic Chemistry · p. 5

06 · 不同框，也能有联系06 · Together, Apart

不在同一个镜头里，怎样仍让人觉得他们属于彼此？How can separate shots still imply a bond?

人物不可能一直在一起；单独拍摄时，仍可维持整部电影中的关系。

Characters can’t be together all the time, but chemistry between characters is still communicated when shot individually. Here are techniques to maintain the connectivity of characters throughout the duration of a movie.

重复姿势

重复姿势：拍下相似的姿态，暗示人物之间的联系。

Repeated Postures

Filming characters in similar postures suggests a connection or relationship between them.

重复取框

重复取框：用完全相同的构图拍不同人物，暗示主题层面的关联。

Repeated Framing

Filming characters in the same exact composition suggests a thematic connection between them.

贴近的细节

贴近的细节：极近特写可展示只有亲密伴侣才会注意到的细节，让观众更贴近人物及其关系。

Up Close and Personal

Extreme closeups can denote a personal connection by showcasing details that only intimate partners could observe, strengthening the audiences connection to them.

© Film Reframed · Cinematic Chemistry · p. 6

07 · 服装与场面调度07 · Costuming & Mise en Scène

先让他们看起来像来自同一个世界，或完全不同的世界。Make them look as if they share a world—or do not.

场面调度

服装、布景和光线都会影响观众怎样理解人物及其关系。

Mise en Scène

Costumes, set design, and lighting affect how your characters and their relationships are percieved.

服装

服装的颜色、图案、轮廓、风格、细节和标志性特征，能传达习惯与心态，也方便比较不同人物。

Costuming

Costumes communicate a great deal about your character’s habits and frame of mind. Color, pattern, silhouette, style, attention to detail, and signature identifiers are some of the qualities that present a character, and compare them to others in their world.

颜色与图案

颜色与图案：颜色、纹样和材质的相同暗示映照或连接，不同则暗示对立或疏离。《请以你的名字呼唤我》中，两人都穿蓝色条纹短袖有领衬衫，暗示 Elio 在 Oliver 身上看见自己。

Color & Pattern

Differences in color, pattern & texture invite direct comparison. Sameness communicates a reflection or connection. Difference communicates opposition or disconnection. See how in Call Me By Your Name, both characters wear a blue, striped, collared short-sleeved shirt to convey that Elio sees himself in Oliver.

统一造型

统一造型：如同共用一个衣橱，配色统一、材质协调，会让人物显得属于彼此。《鸟笼》里的 Albert 与 Armand 很容易被读成伴侣；不相配的人则可以像在完全不同的商店买衣服。

Unified Styling

You can communicate that characters belong together by unifying their costumes and styling. Think of a partnership wearing clothes from the same closet, with a unified color pallette and compatible textures. In The Birdcage we easily read that Albert & Armand are a married couple. Conversely, characters who don’t belong together might look like they shop at different stores entirely.

群体标记

群体标记：用明确或细微的共同制服，把人物与其他人区别开。《侏罗纪公园》中，Grant 与 Sattler 的饱和色、卷起的袖口和明确腰线，帮助维持伙伴关系的视觉印象。

Group Identifiers

Keep characters connected with subtle or explicit identifiers. Think of them having a shared uniform that sets them apart from all the other characters. In Jurassic Park we stay aware that Dr. Grant & Dr. Sattler are partners with saturate colors, rolled cuffs, and defined waistlines.

轮廓

轮廓：服装外形能传达时代、地点、生活方式和人物异同。《比尔和泰德历险记》中，两位主角的相似轮廓，让他们彼此相连，也区别于苏格拉底、弗洛伊德、成吉思汗和贝多芬。

Silhouettes

Silhouettes have a strong impact on your composition. They communicate a great deal about time, place & lifestyle, and will have a part in communicating similarities or differences between characters. In Bill & Ted’s Excellent Adventure, Bill & Ted maintain very similar silhouettes, keeping them connected to each other and distinct from Socrates, Sigmund Freud, Genghis Khan and Beethoven.

重复选择

重复选择：造型也能像构图一样重复母题。《小妇人》中，Jo 的服装颜色和式样呼应母亲在较早场景中的穿着，同时保留自己的穿法。

Repeated Choices

Just like in your compositions, you can keep characters connected with repeated motifs. Here in Little Women, Jo wears an outfit of similar color and styling to something her mother wore in her own way in a previous scene.

© Film Reframed · Cinematic Chemistry · p. 7

08 · 环境与光线08 · Environment & Lighting

把亲密或冷淡，先写进他们所在的空间。Build intimacy or distance into the space around them.

环境

环境会潜移默化地影响我们对身处其中人物的判断。可用地点和美术设计表达关系。

Environment

Environments shape our subliminal perception of the characters that occupy them. Use the following techniques to communicate aspects of yur character’s relationship through location and production design.

有机形状

有机形状：圆润、自然的线条和形状，容易暗示亲密、人性、放松和安全。

Organic Shapes

Rounded, natural lines and shapes suggest intimacy, humanity, ease and safety.

硬直线

硬直线：直线、角度和坚硬边缘，容易暗示冷淡、抽离和不带感情的立场。

Hard Lines

Linear shapes, angles, and hard edges suggest coldness, removal, and impartiality.

光线

光线：共享一个光源的人物常显得相连；被不同或相反光型照亮的人物，则容易显得彼此对立。图示对比统一与紧张。这些效果仍需要放在具体剧情中理解。

Lighting

Characters which share a single light source often feel connected to each other. Characters lit in different or opposing lighting patterns are suggested to be at odds with each other.

© Film Reframed · Cinematic Chemistry · p. 8

12 · 练习 2–3：把观察变成自己的戏12 · Exercises 2–3: From Observation to Fiction

用真实相遇的细节，替代抽象的“他们很有感觉”。Replace an abstract bond with details from real encounters.

练习 2：回忆真实相遇

练习 2：回忆第一次遇见一个重要人物。在哪种环境？光线与气氛如何？两人站或坐得近还是远？一开始就合拍，还是慢慢熟悉？再回忆最近一次见面，问同样的环境、光线、距离问题，并比较与初次相遇的不同。把记忆写成场景、分镜或概念，再用具体细节加强人物间的化学反应。

2. Recreate a Scene from Your Life

Think of a memory from your own life in which you met someone important to you for the first time. Use the following prompts to recall specific details: What environment did you meet in? What was the lighting and mood of that space? Did you stand or sit close to each other, or far away? Did you hit it off at first, or did you need time to warm up to each other? Now think of the last time you saw this person. What environment were you in? What was the lighting and mood of that space? Did you stand or sit close to each other, or far away? How did this encounter differ from your first memory of the person? Use these memories to write, storyboard, or conceptualize a scene in a film about these events, then use the details you recall to create more chemistry between the characters.

练习 3：从纪录片转向剧情片

练习 3：看一段包含至少两个人的纪录片场景。仔细观察每人的互动，对照本课技巧，找出真实生活里自然出现的模式。记下发现，并把观察转化到自己正在创作的剧情片场景中。

3. Transpose Documentary to Narrative Fiction

Watch a scene from a documentary that involves two or more characters. Carefully observe each person’s interactions, and correlate them to the chemistry techniques from this course. Do you naturally observe any of these trends within the real life scene? Record what you find, and transpose any observations to a scene you are working on in a narrative project.
