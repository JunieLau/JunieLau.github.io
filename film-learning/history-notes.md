# Film history — provenance and editorial notes

Research / editing date: 2026-11-07 (Asia/Shanghai, UTC+8).

All seven supplied PDFs were fully text-extracted and their complete tall-page map renders reviewed. No OCR was necessary. No map image or PDF is redistributed in the site. The main page is an independently paraphrased bilingual editorial course, preserving the principal branches, movements, institutions, filmmakers, and examples—not a claimed word-for-word translation or exhaustive national history. Complete unmodified Chinese pdftotext extractions are downloadable for provenance, including original errors, evaluative claims, and uncertain translated titles; these source records are explicitly not verified course text. Original author/publication remains unidentified.

## Identified corrections, qualifications, and catalog-date variations

### 英国 / United Kingdom

- 《愤怒的回顾》导演应为 Tony Richardson；Richard Burton 是主演。
  - Look Back in Anger is directed by Tony Richardson; Richard Burton stars.
  - Source: https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/reviews/look-back-anger-archive-review-best-young-british-film-our-generation
- 区分 BFI 1933 年成立与 1983 年获皇家特许；Crown 关闭是 1952 年。
  - Distinguish BFI’s founding in 1933 from its 1983 Royal Charter; Crown closed in 1952.
  - Source: https://www.bfi.org.uk/strategy-policy/bfis-royal-charter
  - Source: http://www.screenonline.org.uk/film/id/469778/index.html
- 配额和 Eady Levy 于 1985 年终止；“80 年代以后”包含后来出品，并非全部发生于该十年。
  - Quotas and the Eady Levy ended in 1985; the post-1980s branch includes later releases.
  - Source: http://www.screenonline.org.uk/film/id/1051523/index.html
- 原图《汽车的婚礼》按 Runaway Match / Elopement à la Mode 对应时，导演是 Alf Collins，不是 Esmé Collings；保留译名对应的说明。
  - For the Automobile Wedding / Runaway Match identification, the director is Alf Collins, not Esmé Collings; the translated-title identification is stated explicitly.
  - Source: https://catalog.afi.com/Film/31756-ELOPEMENT-A-LA-MODE

### 德国 / Germany

- 《卡里加里博士的小屋》公映年 1920；《大都会》公映年 1927，不是 1924。
  - Caligari was released in 1920; Metropolis in 1927, not 1924.
  - Source: https://www.bfi.org.uk/lists/10-great-german-expressionist-films
- Riefenstahl 1935 年片名为《意志的胜利》／Triumph of the Will；纠正原图含混的胜利片名。
  - Riefenstahl’s 1935 film is Triumph of the Will; the map’s ambiguous victory title is normalized.
  - Source: https://www.bfi.org.uk/film/9c2d0c54-6f7d-5a5e-94e6-5e9c3dc73b4e/triumph-of-the-will
- Kuratorium 并未在 1976 年解散；官方沿革记述其持续发展及 1982 年改制。
  - Kuratorium was not dissolved in 1976; its official history records continuity and reconstitution in 1982.
  - Source: https://www.kuratorium-junger-film.de/ueber-uns
- 《除夕》有 1923 制作／1924 公映的片目差异，不把原图 1923 武断判错。
  - New Year’s Eve has 1923 production / 1924 release variation; the map’s 1923 is not simply declared wrong.
  - Source: https://www.bfi.org.uk/film/aa6cdabd-1fa7-5e63-be12-cef6a0bf5963/sylvester

### 意大利 / Italy

- LUCE 起源应为 1924，不是 1923。
  - LUCE originated in 1924, not 1923.
  - Source: https://www.archivioluce.com/chi-siamo/
- 《大地在波动》1948，不是 1947；《罗马十一时》1952，不是 1951。
  - La terra trema is dated 1948, not 1947; Rome 11:00 is 1952, not 1951.
  - Source: https://www.bfi.org.uk/lists/10-great-italian-neorealist-films
- 《红色沙漠》是 Antonioni 首部彩色长片，不是世界第一部彩色电影。
  - Red Desert is Antonioni’s first color feature, not the world’s first color film.
  - Source: https://www.criterion.com/films/1454-red-desert
- “不表演、不写作、放弃调度”等绝对说法已改为有差异的实践特征。
  - Absolute claims of no acting, writing, or mise-en-scène are replaced by varied working tendencies.
  - Source: https://www.bfi.org.uk/lists/10-great-italian-neorealist-films

### 日本 / Japan

- 东宝集团前身 1932、东宝映画 1937；原图 1934 不能直接充当成立日期。
  - Toho’s predecessor dates to 1932 and Toho Eiga to 1937; 1934 is not a substitute founding date.
  - Source: https://www.toho.co.jp/en/company/history/
- 《罗生门》电影年 1950，威尼斯获奖年 1951。
  - Rashomon’s film year is 1950; its Venice award is from 1951.
  - Source: https://www.criterion.com/current/posts/920-rashomon
- “森田洪幸”应为“森田宏幸”；女形不等于艺伎。
  - Hiroyuki Morita’s name uses 宏, not 洪; onnagata should not be equated with geisha.
  - Source: https://www.ghibli.jp/works/baron/
- 广义新浪潮“止于 1983”保留为分期意见；不当作公认终止日。个别不明译名只存原文档，不猜造外文片名。
  - A broad New Wave ending in 1983 is treated as a periodization opinion, not consensus. Unresolved translated titles remain in the source record without invented identifications.
  - Source: https://www.bfi.org.uk/features/where-begin-japanese-new-wave

### 法国 / France

- 《西班牙的节日》导演是 Germaine Dulac，Delluc 是编剧；1919 年拍摄、1920 年公映。
  - La Fête espagnole is directed by Germaine Dulac and written by Delluc; shot in 1919, released in 1920.
  - Source: https://www.cinematheque.fr/henri/film/48275-la-fete-espagnole-germaine-dulac-1920/
- 《淘气鬼》是 1957 年短片，不是 1958 年长片处女作。
  - Les Mistons is a 1957 short, not a 1958 feature debut.
  - Source: https://www.bfi.org.uk/film/ab080bd6-ba91-5faf-811e-b94fe4d0515b/les-mistons
- 《悲哀与怜悯》是 Marcel Ophuls，不是 Chris Marker；《夏日纪事》保留 Morin 共同导演。
  - The Sorrow and the Pity is by Marcel Ophuls, not Chris Marker; Morin’s codirecting credit is retained for Chronicle of a Summer.
  - Source: https://www.bfi.org.uk/film/f6840020-20a0-5e27-9176-aead1339823c/the-sorrow-and-the-pity
  - Source: https://www.bfi.org.uk/film/1a0f6074-ee15-5624-bf02-239ad74edae9/chronicle-of-a-summer
- 《57 号唱机》校正为 Disque 957；《走卒们》《皇家华尔兹》的确切原文片名暂不确定，不在正文猜造。
  - Disque 957 replaces the incomplete 57 label; the exact original titles behind the map’s Zou zu men and Royal Waltz remain unconfirmed and are not guessed in the course.
  - Source: https://www.cinematheque.fr/film/31245.html

### 美国 / United States

- 《一个国家的诞生》1915，不是 1902；《北方的纳努克》1922，不是 1916。
  - The Birth of a Nation is 1915, not 1902; Nanook of the North is 1922, not 1916.
  - Source: https://www.loc.gov/item/95522190/
- Pat Sullivan／Otto Messmer 对应 Felix the Cat（菲力猫），不是 Garfield（加菲猫）。
  - Pat Sullivan / Otto Messmer are associated with Felix the Cat, not Garfield.
  - Source: https://id.loc.gov/authorities/names/n85031301.html
- 《乐队上场》1940 导演为 Busby Berkeley，不是 Minnelli；《夜困摩天岭》1941，不是 1940。
  - Strike Up the Band (1940) is directed by Busby Berkeley, not Minnelli; High Sierra is 1941, not 1940.
  - Source: https://catalog.afi.com/Film/6816-STRIKE-UP-THE-BAND
  - Source: https://catalog.afi.com/Film/26738-HIGH-SIERRA
- 《公民凯恩》不应省略 Herman J. Mankiewicz 的共同编剧；Michael Snow／《波长》为加拿大，不因放在美国章节而改籍。
  - Retain Herman J. Mankiewicz’s cowriting credit for Citizen Kane; Michael Snow / Wavelength are Canadian despite the US chapter placement.
  - Source: https://cfe.tiff.net/content/films/wavelength
  - Source: https://catalog.afi.com/Film/27624-CITIZEN-KANE
- “多数 race-film 公司白人所有”可有史料支持，但不能抹去 Micheaux 等黑人作者；这是补足，不是把原图的“多数”偷换成“全部”。
  - Majority white ownership of race-film companies has historical support, but must not erase Micheaux and other Black authors; this is qualification, not misreading most as all.
  - Source: https://www.bfi.org.uk/features/short-history-black-us-indie-cinema
- 《化身博士》有 1931 首映／1932 正式发行差异，原图 1932 不判为错误；《将军号》也有 1926／1927 片目差异。
  - Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde has 1931 premiere / 1932 general-release variation; the map’s 1932 is not declared wrong. The General also has 1926 / 1927 catalog variation.
  - Source: https://catalog.afi.com/Film/7380-DR.-JEKYLL-AND-MR.-HYDE

### 苏联／东欧 / USSR / Eastern Europe

- 帝俄在 1908 年已有《斯坚卡·拉辛》等剧情制作，不能从 1920 年代才计算电影起点。
  - Imperial Russia had narrative production such as Stenka Razin in 1908; the starting point is not only the 1920s.
  - Source: https://www.istanbulmodern.org/en/film/stenka-razin
- 1925 年《波将金号》纪念 1905 年革命二十周年，不是 1917 年革命二十周年；Vertov 不随意列成 Kuleshov 学生。
  - Potemkin (1925) marks twenty years after 1905, not 1917; Vertov is not simply assigned as a Kuleshov pupil.
  - Source: https://www.bfi.org.uk/lists/10-great-soviet-films-1920s
- 1953 年 Stalin 去世，1956 年二十大；《士兵之歌》1959，不是 1958。
  - Stalin’s death was in 1953 and the Twentieth Congress in 1956; Ballad of a Soldier is 1959, not 1958.
  - Source: https://www.bfi.org.uk/film/8fd5645f-ac78-52a7-b705-4d5ad5476cac/ballada-o-soldate
  - Source: https://digitalarchive.wilsoncenter.org/topics/nikita-khrushchev-collection
- 苏联不等于俄罗斯；东欧国家也不等于苏联。《莫斯科不相信眼泪》1980；“1999 完全被占领”不作为统计事实。
  - USSR is not synonymous with Russia, nor Eastern Europe with USSR. Moscow Does Not Believe in Tears is 1980; the 1999 total-occupation claim is not treated as a statistic.
  - Source: https://www.peramuseum.org/film/moscow-does-not-believe-in-tears/1078/190

## Additional checking references / caveats

- BFI founding (1933): https://www.bfi.org.uk/about-bfi ; Charter (1983) is a different event.
- EMB / GPO / Crown chronology: http://www.screenonline.org.uk/film/id/513720/index.html and http://www.screenonline.org.uk/film/id/469778/index.html .
- Toho: official history differentiates the 1932 predecessor, 1937 Toho Eiga, and 1943 merger/name change; 1934 is a theater-opening date, not an all-purpose corporate founding date.
- Beyond Silence: https://www.filmportal.de/film/jenseits-der-stille_ebf7ca3e47da4bb0b7027070c8ac11b8 — 1995/96 production, German premiere 19 December 1996; later territorial releases do not replace this.
- Citizen Kane’s shared screenplay credit: https://catalog.afi.com/Film/27624-CITIZEN-KANE; the credited names are Herman J. Mankiewicz and Orson Welles.
- Some sources use production, premiere, or general-release dates differently (Caligari, Sylvester, La Fête espagnole, Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde, The General, Within Our Gates). Differences are not automatically errors. Film year / festival year are explicitly separated for Rashomon and The Cranes Are Flying.
- Original absolutist claims about sole inventors, unacted / unwritten neorealism, a uniformly apolitical New Wave, compulsory three-person vérité crews, fixed movement endpoints, total Hollywood market occupation, and timeless corporate ownership are qualified rather than repeated as facts.
- Eastern Europe is retained as the source map’s branch, not recategorized as one country or Russia. Soviet republics retain distinctions; later Soviet and post-Soviet histories are not conflated.
- Unresolved translated titles (including the French map’s 走卒们 / 皇家华尔兹 and some experimental titles) remain in the original records; no authoritative English title is invented for them.
- This is light fact-checking, not a certification of every date or every interpretation in the source maps.
