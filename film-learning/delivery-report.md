## Interpretation and bibliography corrections

- Modern Monomyth was not uniquely identifiable as a book from the supplied label. The page explicitly uses an editorial umbrella: modern screen adaptations and critiques after Campbell, anchored in Vogler, compared with Harmon’s Story Circle, Lawrence/Jewett’s American monomyth, and Murdock’s Heroine’s Journey. It does not invent a title, author, or ISBN.
- Campbell’s foundational text is The Hero with a Thousand Faces (1949); The Hero’s Journey also names a 1990 conversation/biographical collection.
- Aristotle described complete action; he did not prescribe screenplay acts or percentages. Field’s later model is distinguished from Poetics.
- Freytag’s original Climax is a central turning group, not simply a late-film finale.
- Verified Jonathan Glazer BRAVIA work: Paint (2006). Balls (2005) is Nicolai Fuglsig, not Glazer. Sources: https://www.academyfilms.com/portfolio/sony-paint ; https://www.luerzersarchive.com/sony-bravia-2005-balls/ .

### 英国 / United Kingdom

- Look Back in Anger is directed by Tony Richardson; Richard Burton stars. https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/reviews/look-back-anger-archive-review-best-young-british-film-our-generation
- Distinguish BFI’s founding in 1933 from its 1983 Royal Charter; Crown closed in 1952. https://www.bfi.org.uk/strategy-policy/bfis-royal-charter ; http://www.screenonline.org.uk/film/id/469778/index.html
- Quotas and the Eady Levy ended in 1985; the post-1980s branch includes later releases. http://www.screenonline.org.uk/film/id/1051523/index.html
- For the Automobile Wedding / Runaway Match identification, the director is Alf Collins, not Esmé Collings; the translated-title identification is stated explicitly. https://catalog.afi.com/Film/31756-ELOPEMENT-A-LA-MODE

### 德国 / Germany

- Caligari was released in 1920; Metropolis in 1927, not 1924. https://www.bfi.org.uk/lists/10-great-german-expressionist-films
- Riefenstahl’s 1935 film is Triumph of the Will; the map’s ambiguous victory title is normalized. https://www.bfi.org.uk/film/9c2d0c54-6f7d-5a5e-94e6-5e9c3dc73b4e/triumph-of-the-will
- Kuratorium was not dissolved in 1976; its official history records continuity and reconstitution in 1982. https://www.kuratorium-junger-film.de/ueber-uns
- New Year’s Eve has 1923 production / 1924 release variation; the map’s 1923 is not simply declared wrong. https://www.bfi.org.uk/film/aa6cdabd-1fa7-5e63-be12-cef6a0bf5963/sylvester

### 意大利 / Italy

- LUCE originated in 1924, not 1923. https://www.archivioluce.com/chi-siamo/
- La terra trema is dated 1948, not 1947; Rome 11:00 is 1952, not 1951. https://www.bfi.org.uk/lists/10-great-italian-neorealist-films
- Red Desert is Antonioni’s first color feature, not the world’s first color film. https://www.criterion.com/films/1454-red-desert
- Absolute claims of no acting, writing, or mise-en-scène are replaced by varied working tendencies. https://www.bfi.org.uk/lists/10-great-italian-neorealist-films

### 日本 / Japan

- Toho’s predecessor dates to 1932 and Toho Eiga to 1937; 1934 is not a substitute founding date. https://www.toho.co.jp/en/company/history/
- Rashomon’s film year is 1950; its Venice award is from 1951. https://www.criterion.com/current/posts/920-rashomon
- Hiroyuki Morita’s name uses 宏, not 洪; onnagata should not be equated with geisha. https://www.ghibli.jp/works/baron/
- A broad New Wave ending in 1983 is treated as a periodization opinion, not consensus. Unresolved translated titles remain in the source record without invented identifications. https://www.bfi.org.uk/features/where-begin-japanese-new-wave

### 法国 / France

- La Fête espagnole is directed by Germaine Dulac and written by Delluc; shot in 1919, released in 1920. https://www.cinematheque.fr/henri/film/48275-la-fete-espagnole-germaine-dulac-1920/
- Les Mistons is a 1957 short, not a 1958 feature debut. https://www.bfi.org.uk/film/ab080bd6-ba91-5faf-811e-b94fe4d0515b/les-mistons
- The Sorrow and the Pity is by Marcel Ophuls, not Chris Marker; Morin’s codirecting credit is retained for Chronicle of a Summer. https://www.bfi.org.uk/film/f6840020-20a0-5e27-9176-aead1339823c/the-sorrow-and-the-pity ; https://www.bfi.org.uk/film/1a0f6074-ee15-5624-bf02-239ad74edae9/chronicle-of-a-summer
- Disque 957 replaces the incomplete 57 label; the exact original titles behind the map’s Zou zu men and Royal Waltz remain unconfirmed and are not guessed in the course. https://www.cinematheque.fr/film/31245.html

### 美国 / United States

- The Birth of a Nation is 1915, not 1902; Nanook of the North is 1922, not 1916. https://www.loc.gov/item/95522190/
- Pat Sullivan / Otto Messmer are associated with Felix the Cat, not Garfield. https://id.loc.gov/authorities/names/n85031301.html
- Strike Up the Band (1940) is directed by Busby Berkeley, not Minnelli; High Sierra is 1941, not 1940. https://catalog.afi.com/Film/6816-STRIKE-UP-THE-BAND ; https://catalog.afi.com/Film/26738-HIGH-SIERRA
- Retain Herman J. Mankiewicz’s cowriting credit for Citizen Kane; Michael Snow / Wavelength are Canadian despite the US chapter placement. https://cfe.tiff.net/content/films/wavelength ; https://catalog.afi.com/Film/27624-CITIZEN-KANE
- Majority white ownership of race-film companies has historical support, but must not erase Micheaux and other Black authors; this is qualification, not misreading most as all. https://www.bfi.org.uk/features/short-history-black-us-indie-cinema
- Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde has 1931 premiere / 1932 general-release variation; the map’s 1932 is not declared wrong. The General also has 1926 / 1927 catalog variation. https://catalog.afi.com/Film/7380-DR.-JEKYLL-AND-MR.-HYDE

### 苏联／东欧 / USSR / Eastern Europe

- Imperial Russia had narrative production such as Stenka Razin in 1908; the starting point is not only the 1920s. https://www.istanbulmodern.org/en/film/stenka-razin
- Potemkin (1925) marks twenty years after 1905, not 1917; Vertov is not simply assigned as a Kuleshov pupil. https://www.bfi.org.uk/lists/10-great-soviet-films-1920s
- Stalin’s death was in 1953 and the Twentieth Congress in 1956; Ballad of a Soldier is 1959, not 1958. https://www.bfi.org.uk/film/8fd5645f-ac78-52a7-b705-4d5ad5476cac/ballada-o-soldate ; https://digitalarchive.wilsoncenter.org/topics/nikita-khrushchev-collection
- USSR is not synonymous with Russia, nor Eastern Europe with USSR. Moscow Does Not Believe in Tears is 1980; the 1999 total-occupation claim is not treated as a statistic. https://www.peramuseum.org/film/moscow-does-not-believe-in-tears/1078/190

## Original cinematography tables: complete concise audit

Manufacturer specifications are distinguished from subjective style associations. Counts: 8 confirmed, 16 corrected/qualified, 6 unverifiable aesthetic proposals. Full bilingual original-claim / finding detail is in `fact-check.md`.

- **A01 · ARRI ALEXA 35 — correct**: Confirmed Super 35, 4608 × 3164, and manufacturer-rated 17 stops. Skin and highlights are manufacturer descriptions. Genre suitability is not an objectively exclusive fact and remains a test proposal. https://www.arri.com/en/cine-systems/cine-cameras/legacy-cine-cameras/alexa-35

- **A02 · ALEXA Mini LF / LF — corrected**: Format and wider view at equal focal length are valid. Shallower depth requires stated comparison conditions, such as matched framing, viewpoint, and aperture. Compactness belongs to Mini LF, not LF. Both use a 36.70 × 25.54 mm, 4448 × 3096 sensor. https://www.arri.com/en/cine-systems/cine-cameras/alexa-mini-lf

- **A03 · ARRI ALEXA 65 — corrected**: Confirmed digital 65 format, 54.12 × 25.58 mm, 6560 × 3100; not 65 mm film. “Extreme” and “epic” are not measurable conclusions. Depth and perspective do not come from the sensor alone; reframed as composition and viewpoint exercises. https://www.arrirental.com/en/cameras/digital-65-mm-cameras/alexa-65 ; https://theasc.com/article/optics-oppenheimer-sasaki/

- **A04 · Sony VENICE 2 — corrected**: Confirmed 8640 × 5760 and dual base ISO 800 / 3200 for the 8.6K version. Specify the version: the 6K sensor is 500 / 2500. Low-light performance is a manufacturer claim; night scenes still require exposure and lighting tests. https://sony-cinematography.com/venice2/

- **A05 · RED V-RAPTOR [X] — corrected**: Confirmed 8K and global shutter. 120 fps is for 8K 17:9; 8K 2.4:1 reaches 150 fps, with higher rates at lower resolutions. Removes rolling-shutter skew, not all motion blur or artifacts. Distinguish [X] from non-[X] models. https://www.reddigitalcinema.com/compare-specs ; https://www.reddigitalcinema.com/stories/v-raptor-x-launch

- **A06 · 35 mm VISION3 — corrected**: 500T is tungsten-balanced; 250D / 50D are daylight-balanced, not restricted to night or day. Stocks confirmed. Grain and skin color depend on exposure, processing, scan, and grade; warmth is not guaranteed. https://www.kodak.com/en/motion/products/camera-films/

- **A07 · 16 mm 胶片 / film — unverifiable**: 16 mm is a format, not a uniform emulsion. A smaller negative can make grain more visible under comparable conditions. Intimacy, documentary feel, and memory cannot be verified as equipment properties; retained as expressive exercises. Nobody Knows offers a Super 16 case study. https://www.kodak.com/en/motion/products/camera-films/ ; https://www.indiewire.com/features/general/talking-about-nobody-knows-with-a-magician-of-the-cinema-hirokazu-kore-eda-78418/

- **L01 · ARRI Signature Prime — corrected**: Confirmed 16 focal lengths and range. Correct uniform T1.8: 200 mm is T2.5; 280 mm is T2.8. Warm skin and bokeh are official rendering descriptions, not guarantees under every light. https://www.arri.com/en/cine-lenses/signature-lenses/signature-primes-zooms/signature-primes

- **L02 · ARRI Master Prime — correct**: Technical intent matches ARRI: 16 lenses, 12–150 mm, T1.3, emphasizing contrast, geometric control, and minimal breathing. “Cool sci-fi” is not an optical fact and remains a creative suggestion. https://www.arri.com/en/cine-lenses/arri-zeiss-fujinon-lenses/master-primes/master-primes

- **L03 · Cooke S4/i — corrected**: Confirmed S35, 18 regular focal lengths and range; 300 mm is T2.8, not T2. Current table also lists a 65SF variant, not another distinct focal length. Cooke Look is official wording; period suitability is an aesthetic proposal. https://cookeoptics.com/lens/s4-i-prime/

- **L04 · Cooke Panchro/i Classic — corrected**: The historical lineage is officially supported, but Panchro/i Classic is a modern redesign, not glass manufactured then. S35 and FF versions each span 12 focal lengths, 18–152 mm. Contrast, glow, and period associations require testing rather than universal claims. https://cookeoptics.com/lens/panchro-classic-i-s35/ ; https://cookeoptics.com/lens/cooke-panchro-classic-i-ff/

- **L05 · ZEISS Supreme / Radiance — corrected**: Separate the families: regular Supreme has 14 lenses, 15–200 mm; 15 / 150 are T1.8, 200 T2.2, the rest T1.5. Radiance has 11 lenses, 18–135 mm, all T1.5. T* blue and controlled flaring are confirmed, but behavior depends on lighting and flags, not an automatic blue filter. https://www.zeiss.com/photonics-and-optics/en/cinematography/lenses/supreme-prime-lenses.html ; https://www.zeiss.com/photonics-and-optics/en/cinematography/lenses/supreme-prime-radiance-lenses.html

- **L06 · Leica / Leitz SUMMILUX-C — corrected**: Clarified current Leitz branding and specs: 12 lenses, 16–135 mm, T1.4, 33 mm image circle, designed for Super 35. Clarity, flat field, and gentle transitions are officially described; “moderate contrast / cool urban” is not a universal measurable conclusion. https://www.leitz-cine.com/product/summilux-c

- **L07 · 2× 变形镜头 / anamorphics — corrected**: These are common tendencies, not guaranteed in every 2× lens, aperture, or light. 2× is a horizontal squeeze ratio, not automatic 2.39:1; the result depends on sensor window and cropping. Epic scale is interpretation. https://atlaslensco.com/products/orion-series-anamorphic-lenses ; https://cookeoptics.com/lens/anamorphic-i-s35/

- **M01 · 古装宫廷／烛火 / period candlelight — unverifiable**: Plausible test package, not a provable genre prescription. Clarified 35 / 50 mm as actual prime options, then added coverage, candle, and exposure tests. https://www.arri.com/en/cine-systems/cine-cameras/legacy-cine-cameras/alexa-35 ; https://cookeoptics.com/lens/s4-i-prime/

- **M02 · 古装江湖／大场面 / wuxia — corrected**: Missing lens family, coverage, and window. S35 2× lenses need not cover LF; Orion requires a suitable S35 window. Stabilizers also need full-payload and balance checks. The genre match is not objectively verifiable. https://www.arri.com/en/cine-systems/cine-cameras/alexa-mini-lf ; https://atlaslensco.com/products/orion-series-anamorphic-lenses

- **M03 · 现代都市写实 / urban realism — unverifiable**: Equipment and focal length exist; package is a creative proposal. Brand cannot prove realism. Added format / viewpoint conditions and warned that handheld is not sufficient for realism. https://www.arri.com/en/cine-systems/cine-cameras/alexa-mini-lf ; https://www.arri.com/en/cine-lenses/signature-lenses/signature-primes-zooms/signature-primes

- **M04 · 霓虹赛博 / neon cyber mood — unverifiable**: High base ISO and controlled flare have product support; genre suitability is advice. Specified 8.6K 800 / 3200 and added neon-clipping and source-angle tests. Control is not a menu switch. https://sony-cinematography.com/venice2/ ; https://www.zeiss.com/photonics-and-optics/en/cinematography/lenses/supreme-prime-radiance-lenses.html

- **M05 · 悬疑／精确 / suspense — unverifiable**: A testable technical combination, but suspense and coolness arise from information, blocking, and time, not a specification. Retained as a labeled original exercise. https://www.arri.com/en/cine-systems/cine-cameras/legacy-cine-cameras/alexa-35 ; https://www.arri.com/en/cine-lenses/arri-zeiss-fujinon-lenses/master-primes/master-primes

- **M06 · 民国时代记忆 / period memory — unverifiable**: A test proposal; modern lenses and film do not automatically reproduce an era. Added color, wardrobe, exposure, processing, and scan tests rather than presenting association as historical fact. https://www.kodak.com/en/motion/products/camera-films/ ; https://cookeoptics.com/lens/panchro-classic-i-s35/

- **M07 · 史诗战争／一镜到底 / continuous war film — corrected**: 1917 is a real precedent, but neither literally one focal length nor one unbroken take: mainly 40 mm, plus 35 / 47 mm, multiple rigs, and editorial joins. Reframed as a case to study, not a genre requirement. https://www.arri.com/news-en/the-immersive-camera-movement-of-1917-

- **D01 · 库布里克 / Kubrick — correct**: Supported by The Shining (DP John Alcott; Steadicam operated by Garrett Brown). Not his sole style across all films; Barry Lyndon adds slow zooms and painterly composition as a contrast. https://theasc.com/article/flashback-the-shining/ ; https://theasc.com/article/flashback-barry-lyndon/

- **D02 · 诺兰 / Nolan — corrected**: Too broad to verify across a career. Anchored to Oppenheimer with Hoyte van Hoytema: large format also serves intimate close-ups, not only environmental scale, with documented custom optics. https://theasc.com/article/optics-oppenheimer-sasaki/ ; https://www.kodak.com/en/motion/blog-post/oppenheimer/

- **D03 · 韦斯·安德森 / Wes Anderson — correct**: Consistent with reporting on The Grand Budapest Hotel and Robert Yeoman. Scoped to the film, not a claim that every shot is centered or overhead. https://theasc.com/news/robert-yeoman-asc-lifetime-achievement-award/ ; https://mande.net/btl/awards/contender-cinematographer-robert-yeoman-the-grand-budapest-hotel

- **D04 · 芬奇 / Fincher — correct**: Supported by Gone Girl production reporting (Jeff Cronenweth). Added Mank (Erik Messerschmidt) to distinguish projects and collaborators. A style summary is not a substitute for scene analysis. https://theasc.com/article/ac-gallery-gone-girl/ ; https://la411.com/blog/post/gone-girl-cinematographer-jeff-cronenweth ; https://theasc.com/video/clubhouse-conversations-mank/

- **D05 · 维伦纽瓦 / Villeneuve — correct**: A reasonable starting observation for Blade Runner 2049 (Roger Deakins) and Dune (Greig Fraser), not a claim that every scene is slow or shares one lighting scheme. https://theasc.com/article/deakins-blade-runner-2049/ ; https://theasc.com/article/dune-fear-is-the-mind-killer/

- **D06 · 王家卫 / Wong Kar-wai — corrected**: Not universal lens settings. Uses In the Mood for Love’s occlusion, frames, and time as a case; credit both Christopher Doyle and Mark Lee Ping-Bing. Motion smear involves frame rate, shutter, and post, not wide-angle lenses alone. https://www.bfi.org.uk/features/mood-love-wong-kar-wai-emotion ; https://shop.bfi.org.uk/in-the-mood-for-love-blu-ray/

- **D09 · 侯孝贤 / Hou Hsiao-hsien — corrected**: These are not universal rules. Flowers of Shanghai (Mark Lee Ping-Bing) uses sustained slow movement and lamp / supplemental lighting.
