# 摄影原表核对 / Cinematography source-table audit

核对日期 / Checked: 2026-11-07, China Standard Time (UTC+8).

技术规格依据厂家；审美建议不能当成事实。 / Specifications follow manufacturers; aesthetic proposals are not facts.

## A01 · ARRI ALEXA 35 — correct

原说法：S35 4.6K、17 档；肤色自然、高光柔和；写实／古装烛火。

Original: S35 4.6K, 17 stops; natural skin and gentle highlights; realism / period candlelight.

核对：确认 S35、4608 × 3164 与厂家 17 档；肤色与高光属于厂家描述。题材适用性不是可验证的排他结论，保留为试拍建议。

Finding: Confirmed Super 35, 4608 × 3164, and manufacturer-rated 17 stops. Skin and highlights are manufacturer descriptions. Genre suitability is not an objectively exclusive fact and remains a test proposal.

- https://www.arri.com/en/cine-systems/cine-cameras/legacy-cine-cameras/alexa-35

## A02 · ALEXA Mini LF / LF — corrected

原说法：大画幅、同焦段视野更宽、景深更浅；机身小，适合稳定器。

Original: Large format, wider view at the same focal length, shallower depth; small body for stabilizers.

核对：画幅与相同焦距下更宽视角成立；“景深更浅”须固定构图、机位与光圈等比较条件，不是无条件属性。“机身小”只适用于 Mini LF；两者同用 36.70 × 25.54 mm、4448 × 3096 传感器。

Finding: Format and wider view at equal focal length are valid. Shallower depth requires stated comparison conditions, such as matched framing, viewpoint, and aperture. Compactness belongs to Mini LF, not LF. Both use a 36.70 × 25.54 mm, 4448 × 3096 sensor.

- https://www.arri.com/en/cine-systems/cine-cameras/alexa-mini-lf

## A03 · ARRI ALEXA 65 — corrected

原说法：65 mm 级大画幅；极致细节与纵深、史诗风景／广角近景。

Original: 65 mm-class large format; extreme detail and depth, epic landscapes / wide-angle close-ups.

核对：确认数字 65 格式、54.12 × 25.58 mm、6560 × 3100；不是 65 mm 胶片。“极致”和“史诗”不可客观验证；纵深与透视不是传感器单独决定，改为构图与机位练习。

Finding: Confirmed digital 65 format, 54.12 × 25.58 mm, 6560 × 3100; not 65 mm film. “Extreme” and “epic” are not measurable conclusions. Depth and perspective do not come from the sensor alone; reframed as composition and viewpoint exercises.

- https://www.arrirental.com/en/cameras/digital-65-mm-cameras/alexa-65
- https://theasc.com/article/optics-oppenheimer-sasaki/

## A04 · Sony VENICE 2 — corrected

原说法：全画幅 8.6K、双原生 ISO 800 / 3200；低光纯净、实景光源夜戏。

Original: Full-frame 8.6K, dual native ISO 800 / 3200; clean low light, practical-lit nights.

核对：确认 8.6K 版本的 8640 × 5760、双基础 ISO 800 / 3200；需写明版本，VENICE 2 的 6K 传感器为 500 / 2500。低光表现是厂家主张，夜戏仍需曝光与灯光测试。

Finding: Confirmed 8640 × 5760 and dual base ISO 800 / 3200 for the 8.6K version. Specify the version: the 6K sensor is 500 / 2500. Low-light performance is a manufacturer claim; night scenes still require exposure and lighting tests.

- https://sony-cinematography.com/venice2/

## A05 · RED V-RAPTOR [X] — corrected

原说法：8K 全局快门，最高 120 fps；高速动作无果冻效应，打斗／升格。

Original: 8K global shutter, maximum 120 fps; high-speed action without jelly effects, fights / slow motion.

核对：确认 8K 与全局快门。120 fps 限于 8K 17:9；8K 2.4:1 为 150 fps，低分辨率还可更高。可避免滚动快门几何偏斜，不保证没有运动模糊或其他伪影；[X] 与无 [X] 型号不可混写。

Finding: Confirmed 8K and global shutter. 120 fps is for 8K 17:9; 8K 2.4:1 reaches 150 fps, with higher rates at lower resolutions. Removes rolling-shutter skew, not all motion blur or artifacts. Distinguish [X] from non-[X] models.

- https://www.reddigitalcinema.com/compare-specs
- https://www.reddigitalcinema.com/stories/v-raptor-x-launch

## A06 · 35 mm VISION3 — corrected

原说法：500T 夜景室内；250D / 50D 日景；有机颗粒、温暖肤色。

Original: 500T for night interiors; 250D / 50D for day; organic grain and warm skin.

核对：500T 为钨丝灯平衡、250D / 50D 为日光平衡，不能将 T / D 等同只能夜／日拍摄。确认这些乳剂存在；颗粒与肤色受曝光、冲洗、扫描和调色影响，“温暖”不作恒定保证。

Finding: 500T is tungsten-balanced; 250D / 50D are daylight-balanced, not restricted to night or day. Stocks confirmed. Grain and skin color depend on exposure, processing, scan, and grade; warmth is not guaranteed.

- https://www.kodak.com/en/motion/products/camera-films/

## A07 · 16 mm 胶片 / film — unverifiable

原说法：颗粒明显、粗粝亲近；纪实感／回忆段落。

Original: Visible grain, raw intimacy; documentary feel / memories.

核对：16 mm 是格式而非统一乳剂。较小底片在可比条件下可使颗粒更显著；“亲近、纪实、回忆”无法作为设备参数证实，保留为表达练习。《无人知晓》的 Super 16 实践可作案例。

Finding: 16 mm is a format, not a uniform emulsion. A smaller negative can make grain more visible under comparable conditions. Intimacy, documentary feel, and memory cannot be verified as equipment properties; retained as expressive exercises. Nobody Knows offers a Super 16 case study.

- https://www.kodak.com/en/motion/products/camera-films/
- https://www.indiewire.com/features/general/talking-about-nobody-knows-with-a-magician-of-the-cinema-hirokazu-kore-eda-78418/

## L05 · ZEISS Supreme / Radiance — corrected

## L07 · 2× 变形镜头 / anamorphics — corrected

原说法：椭圆焦外、水平拉丝眩光；宽银幕史诗感、大场面气场。

Original: Oval bokeh, horizontal streaks; widescreen epic scale.

核对：这些是常见但不是所有 2× 镜头、光圈和光源下都保证出现的特征。2× 是横向压缩比，不自动生成固定 2.39:1；解挤宽高比取决于记录窗口和裁切。史诗感是审美解读。

Finding: These are common tendencies, not guaranteed in every 2× lens, aperture, or light. 2× is a horizontal squeeze ratio, not automatic 2.39:1; the result depends on sensor window and cropping. Epic scale is interpretation.

- https://atlaslensco.com/products/orion-series-anamorphic-lenses
- https://cookeoptics.com/lens/anamorphic-i-s35/

核对：“固定、远景、自然光”不是统一规则。《海上花》（李屏宾）有持续的缓慢移动与灯烛／辅助照明；改为长时段、空间关系与注意力转移的研究。

Finding: These are not universal rules. Flowers of Shanghai (Mark Lee Ping-Bing) uses sustained slow movement and lamp / supplemental lighting. Reframed around duration, spatial relations, and shifts of attention.
