理解色彩Understanding Color

01 · 理解色彩01 · Understanding color

颜色是电影视觉语言里极具感染力的表达手段。你越了解观众在科学层面上怎样看见、理解颜色，就越能掌控这个强大的讲故事工具。

Color is an evocative communicator in the visual language of film. The more you know about how audiences see and interpret color from a scientific perspective, the more adept you will be at controlling this powerful aspect of your storytelling.

02 · 我们怎样看见颜色？02 · How do we see color?

1. 白光包含七种波长的颜色：红、橙、黄、绿、蓝、靛、紫（ROYGBIV）。

1. White light contains seven wavelengths of color (Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Indigo, and Violet - or ROYGBIV).

2. 这些波长照到物体（比如苹果）上时，物体会吸收一部分波长、反射另一部分。被反射回来的色光，就是我们以为的物体颜色。（苹果吸收了橙黄绿蓝靛紫，只反射红，所以我们看到它是红的。）物体反射所有波长就显白，吸收所有波长就显黑。物体本身没有固有的颜色——它们只是反射色光。

2. When these wavelengths hit an object (such as an apple), the object will selectively absorb some of the wavelengths and reflect others. The colored light that is reflected from the object is the color we perceive the object to be. (In the case of an apple, it absorbs OYGBIV, and reflects R, so we see it as red.) If an object reflects all wavelengths of light, it will appear white. If it absorbs all wavelengths of light, it will appear black. Objects themselves do not have an inherent color - they only reflect colored light.

3. 物体反射的光由我们眼睛里的视杆细胞和视锥细胞来解读。视锥细胞有三种：红、绿、蓝。视锥细胞需要足够多的光才能分辨颜色，所以弱光下靠视杆细胞帮我们看。视杆细胞对某些波长不敏感，这就是为什么夜里或烛光下物体显得不清楚、不饱和。

3. The light that is reflected from the object is interpreted by our eyes through the use of rods and cones. There are three types of cones: Red, Green, and Blue. These cones need a significant amount of light to enter them before they are able to interpret color, so Rods are there to help us see in low light conditions. They are less sensitive to certain wavelengths of color, which is why objects appear less distinct or saturate at night, or by candle light.

4. 大脑总想按颜色给物体归类、命名（比如把蛇果叫红色、把橙子叫橙色），所以常常会错误解读视杆和视锥传来的信息。因此我们必须训练大脑，去观察每个情境里真实呈现的色光，而不是依赖颜色标签。

4. Because our brains want to categorize and identify objects by their color (for instance, calling Red Delicious apples red, or Oranges orange), they will often interpret the information provided by our Rods and Cones incorrectly. This is why we must train our brains to observe the actuality of the colored light being presented to us in each situation, rather than relying on color identifiers.

04 · 固有色04 · Local color

物体既然没有固有颜色，我们怎么称呼它的颜色？

可以用“固有色（Local Color）”来描述用来识别物体的那个颜色。比如蛇果的固有色是红色；但在荧光灯下看这个苹果，它会显得发棕。

所以在片场看监视器里荧光灯下的苹果时，它就是棕色的，但固有色是红色。说“固有色”能避免你脑子里的混乱，因为它提醒你：物体并不总是呈现成它的颜色标签。

在《杀死比尔》的这些画面里，夹克的固有色可以称为黄色；但随光线条件不同，它会显得偏橙、偏棕或偏绿。

How can we refer to the color of objects if they don’t have inherent colors?

We can use the term “Local Color” to describe the identifying color of an object. For instance, a “red delicious” apple will have a local color of Red. However, if we see an apple in fluorescent lighting conditions, it will appear brown.

So, if we are looking at a monitor of the fluorescent lit apple on set, the apple IS brown, but has a local color of red. Saying local color will help avoid confusion in your mind about the perceived color of the object, because you are reminding yourself that the object does not always appear as it is labeled by color identifiers.

In the above images from Kill Bill, we can refer to the local color of the jacket as yellow. However, the jacket will appear orange, brown, or greenish in hue depending on the lighting conditions we view it under..

05 · 光的强弱怎样影响颜色？05 · Light intensity & color

物体受光的表面分成五个部分：高光、中间调、明暗交界的核心阴影、反光和投影。

1. 高光最亮，但因为反射的光太多，颜色饱和度会流失。

2. 中间调能看到最多的颜色：光足够激活视杆和视锥，又不像高光那样过量。

3. 核心阴影会带有场景环境光的颜色。比如场景以蓝色为主，阴影里就会带蓝。

4. 反光带着附近物体反弹过来的光的颜色。比如人物靠近一面粉墙，就会受到粉色的反光——墙吸收了其他波长，只把没吸收的颜色反射到别的物体上。

5. 投影明暗值很深，并带上它所落在的表面的颜色。

对照球体图和上面画面里的光影分布，观察哪里颜色更饱和、哪里更不饱和。

Light on the form of an object is broken into five designating terms. There are highlights, mid-tones, core shadows, reflected light, and cast shadows.

1. Highlights will appear brightest in value, but will lose saturation of color because of the amount of light reflecting off of them.

2. Mid-tones will contain the most amount of perceived color, because they have enough light cast on them to activate our rods and cones, but not an excess of light as the highlights do.

3. Core shadows will contain color elements of the ambient light in the scene. For instance, if the setting of your scene is predominantly blue, the shadows will contain a blue hue.

4. Reflected light will contain the color of light that is bounced from nearby objects. For instance, if you place your subject near a pink wall, they will receive reflected light that is pink. This is because the wall absorbed the other wavelengths of light in the color spectrum, reflecting only the color they did not absorb onto your other subjects.

5. Cast shadows will be dark in value, and take on the coloration of the surface they are cast onto.

06 · 色轮在电影里的用法06 · The color wheel in film

色轮把光谱里每种可感知的颜色分成扇区。越靠近圆心颜色越不饱和，越往外越饱和。

限制你在片中使用色轮上的哪些颜色，就能建立色彩和谐和主观联想。这类限制叫做配色方案：单色、类似色、互补色、三角色、分裂互补色、四角色。

从这些方案里选一种颜色关系，就能确定你选的颜色会好看地互相配合。

The color wheel is divided into segments for each perceivable color in the spectrum. The closer you get to the center of the color wheel, the less saturate the color is. The further toward the edges, the more saturate the color is.

By restricting which colors in the wheel you utilize in your films, you can create color harmonies and subjective associations. These forms of restriction are known as color schemes, labeled here: Monochromatic, Analogous, Complementary, Triad, Split-Complementary, Tetradic.

By selecting a color relationship from one of these color schemes, you can be confident that they colors you select are going to interact together in a pleasing way.

四种配色方案的电影实例：单色、类似色、互补色、三角色。

07 · 练习：规划与观察颜色07 · Exercises

练习：规划和观察片中颜色的 5 步流程

问自己：

exercises — a 5 step process for planning and observing colors in your films

Ask yourself:

01我的环境固有色是什么？
它们决定了反光的颜色。What are my local environment colors?
These will dictate the color of your reflected light.
02我的服装、道具和肤色的固有色是什么？
它们决定了主体与背景之间的配色关系。What are my local costume, prop, and skin tone colors?
These will dictate the color scheme interactions of your subjects and backgrounds.
03我选的灯光会怎样影响这些颜色被看到的样子？
灯的光谱决定物体在镜头里呈现什么颜色。How will my lighting selection affect the perception of these colors?
Their spectrums will determine how the color of your objects appear on camera.
04这些灯的强弱会怎样影响我的颜色？
记住：高光的颜色比中间调更不饱和。How will the intensity of these lights affect my colors?
Remember that highlights contain less saturate color than mid-tones.
05我的调色会怎样支撑这些颜色？
记得用色轮来协调场景里的颜色。How will my color grade support these colors?
Remember to utilize the color wheel to harmonize the colors in your scene.
