先从你想让观众感受到什么开始，再看如何安排人物、空间与镜头。Start with what you want the audience to feel, then arrange people, space and shots.

02 · 镜头序列与情绪覆盖02 · Sequencing & Emotional Coverage

先列出情绪变化，再列全景、中景和特写。List the emotional beats before listing shot sizes.

镜头序列

每个镜头都夹在前一个与后一个镜头之间。电影是连续图像；观众不能自己走动或问人物问题，因此导演必须引导他们看见理解故事所需的信息。前后画面共同提供主题、信息和空间背景；有意识地安排镜头关系，才能让电影连贯、有感染力。

Sequencing

Every shot in a film is sandwiched within the context of the shot immediately before it and the shot immediately after it. Movies are simply a long sequence of individual images. Because the audience can’t walk around the space or ask the characters questions, your role as the director is to guide them to what they need to see to understand the story. The images before and after each image create thematic, informational and geographic context. Bringing intentionality to how one image relates to the next is the power you have to create a movie that is cohesive and affecting.

常规覆盖

常规覆盖：不少导演只拍全景、双人镜头、各自特写等常见角度，为剪辑提供选项。这能降低后期缺镜头的风险，却可能让构图泛化、缺少表达。理解构图与剪辑如何共同工作，就能更有把握地设计独特而又能剪在一起的镜头。图示分别为全景、双人镜头与特写。

Coverage

Many directors shoot solely “coverage.” Coverage is a classic selection of angles within a scene (maybe a wide shot, a two shot and each actor’s close ups.) that provide the editor with options in post-production. This mitigates the risk of not having the images you need when you get to the edit, but leads to generalized, uninspired compositions. The better understanding you have of how the principles of composition and editing work together, you’ll have more opportunities to think outside the box and create shots that are unique and affecting– with the confidence to know they’ll cut together!

情绪覆盖

重新理解覆盖：不要只问有没有拍全角度，而要问有没有覆盖场景的每一个情绪节拍。没有对白，观众仍应该能通过画面理解发生了什么。

Reframing Coverage

Instead of starting a shotlist by selecting shots that “cover” you in the edit, try writing a shot list that provides “emotional coverage”. Ask yourself: have the compositions of the shots in your shotlist covered each emotional beat in your scene? Remember that an image is worth 1000 words, and that the audience should be able to understand what’s happening in a scene with no dialogue at all.

03 · 让观众知道自己在哪里03 · Context: Where Am I?

每换一个角度，观众还能认出空间吗？Can the viewer still understand the space after a change of angle?

观众的三个问题

观众看每个画面时，都会试着回答三个问题：我在哪里？我知道什么？我对此有什么感觉？设计构图时，先保证这些问题能得到回应。

Context

When watching a movie, the viewer’s brain is trying to deduce 3 things to make sense of each image: 1. “where am I?” 2. “what do I know?” 3. “how do I feel about it?” When devising your compositions, start by ensuring these questions are answered.

我在哪里？

房产照片与实地看房的落差，说明二维画面会让人误判三维空间。导演需要在正确时刻提供足够信息，让观众定位；可以使用拍摄角度、空间标志和视线。

“Where Am I?”

Have you ever seen a picture of a house or apartment in a real estate listing, then when you go take a tour it somehow looks totally different than you pictured? That’s what’s happening in your movie! It’s the director’s responsibility to keep the viewer oriented in a 2D representation of a 3D world. We need to give the viewer enough information – and at the right time – to get oriented within the space. We do this with everything from angles, to visual landmarks, to eyelines.

地理关系与方向

用建立镜头、容易识别的空间地标和 180 度线，让观众持续认清空间。

Geography and Orientation

You can establish physical geography by using establishing shots, landmarks, and the 180 line.

建立镜头

进入场景叙事前，先从多个角度展示环境，帮助观众建立方向。

Establishing Shots

Filming multiple angles of your environment before diving into the narrative of a scene will help orient your audience to their surroundings.

空间地标

在后续镜头背景里保留容易记住的位置标志，继续帮助观众认路。

Landmarks

In addition to shooting establishing shots, you can include visually memorable indicators of location in the backgrounds of your scenes.

04 · 轴线与 180 度规则04 · The Axis of Action & the 180 Rule

想让剪辑方向稳定，先把两个人之间的线画出来。Draw the line between the subjects before planning stable screen direction.

180 度规则，用摄影机所处的半圆区域，维持观众对场景地理关系的理解。两主体之间的行动线提供轴线基准，摄影机在同一侧的 180 度区域内取景，通常能保持画面方向的一致性。讲义把 Axis、Line 和 Arc 作教学区分；此处以原图对照，而不把其文字定义当作几何定理。

The 180 Rule refers to the arc of space the camera is placed in to preserve the viewer’s geographic understanding of the world. The 180 Arc is established from The Axis of Action. The Axis of Action is the midpoint on the direct Line of Action between two subjects. Once The Line of Action has been established, every shot must be framed from within The 180 Arc to preserve the viewer’s spatial orientation.

重新建立轴线

重新建立轴线的方法：一，人物在一个连续镜头里跨过原来的线；二，摄影机在连续镜头里跨线；三，新人物进入，与已有的人物建立新行动线。关键是把变化展示给观众，而不是突然让方向反转。

Changing or Establishing a New Axis of Action

A new Line of Action can be established in the following ways: 1. A character moves across the line within a single shot. 2. The camera moves across the line within a single shot. 3. A new character enters the scene and establishes a new line of action with an existing character.

把视线写进分镜表

前期解决：分镜表中加入视线方向说明。清楚的视线计划，是检查画面能否顺利剪接的重要工具。

Reframing Shot Lists

Fix it in preproduction! Consider incorporating eyeline delineations within your shotlist. Understanding an eyeline plan is crucial to ensuring your images will cut together.

© Film Reframed · Composition for Editing · p. 4

05 · 让观众知道什么：焦点与信息05 · What Do I Know? Focus & Information

这一镜是在提出问题，还是给出答案？Is this shot asking a question or delivering an answer?

我知道什么？

讲义建议把每个镜头看成提问或回答：构图时先想清楚它在制造疑问，还是传递信息，再决定如何引导注意力。

“What Do I Know?”

Every shot in a film will either ask a question or provide an answer. When deciding on the composition of a shot, understand if it is asking a question (interrogation) or providing an answer (information). But how do we guide our audience’s attention to the things they need to know?

注意力中心

静态画面的视觉中心，受清晰度、反差和位置影响：眼睛容易看向清楚的主体、反差最大的地方，以及在画面或环境中位置突出的人物。动态影像还增加三种因素：人脸，尤其是有情绪的表情；声音，观众会寻找发声者；运动，移动的主体容易抓住注意力。

The Focal Point

The focal point of an image is determined by: Focus: Our eyes are drawn to a subject in sharp focus. Contrast: Our eyes are drawn to the highest point of contrast. Placement: Our eyes are drawn to a subject that has prominent placement within the frame (or in relation to its environment). The focal point of moving images is determined by the additional elements: Faces: Our eyes are drawn to human faces, particularly one that is expressing emotion. Noise: Noise demands our attention, so our eyes are drawn to the noisemaker within a frame. Movement: Our eyes are drawn to a subject in motion. How does having a focal point change now that we add the context of editing?

移动的注意力中心

移动的注意力中心：镜头前后相接时，焦点会随新画面改变；镜头内摄影机或人物移动，也会改变其位置。

Moving Focal Points

Because each image is preceded and followed by a new one, our focal point changes from shot to shot. A focal point will change location within a shot when the camera moves or the subject moves.

利用焦点制造发现

重新理解焦点：可以故意让主要兴趣点暂时分散观众对关键线索的注意力。当眼睛终于从第一重点转向第二个细节，就产生发现的感觉。

Reframing Focal Points

Consider making the focal point of your composition a misdirection from a key piece of information in a frame. If you do this, you can create a sense of “discovery” when the audience’s eye finally goes from the primary point of interest to the secondary item.

© Film Reframed · Composition for Editing · p. 5

06 · 剪辑点的视线与人物分组06 · Guiding the Eye & Grouping

想让剪辑顺滑，还是让观众感到一次断裂？Should the cut flow smoothly or feel like a break?

引导视线

前一镜结束时视觉中心的位置，会影响转入下一镜时，剪辑点是明显还是不易察觉。

Guiding the Eye

The placement of the focal point within the frame at the end of a shot will be a determining factor of how visible or invisible a cut will be when transitioning to the next shot.

不明显的剪辑

较不明显的剪辑，可加强人物或主体之间的连接，并维持沉浸。

Invisible Cuts

Making the cut between shots feel invisible can help the characters or subjects in each scene feel more connected, and the audience remain immersed.

明显的剪辑

可被感知的剪辑，可用来表现疏离或冲突。

Visible Cuts

Feeling the cut between shots can be helpful to show a disconnection or conflict between characters or subjects.

前后镜头的注意力距离

前后镜头的注意力中心越近，观众的眼睛就越少需要移动。

Distance Between Focal Points

The closer in proximity the focal point of one frame is to the next, the less distance the viewer’s eye has to travel to follow the action.

剪辑前后：注意力中心接近

观众较少意识到自己在观看，流动感更强，更容易沉浸。

Close Proximity Between Focal Points

Close proximity between two focal points after an edit: less awareness of self, maintains flow, building a more immersive experience.

剪辑前后：注意力中心相隔较远

观众更容易意识到自己在观看；体验更不连贯，也更需要主动参与。

Far Proximity Between Focal Points

Far proximity between two focal points after an edit: more awareness of self, creates a more disjointed, interactive experience.

人物分组

分组：场景中人物的空间组织表达关系。同组或在画面同一侧的人，容易呈现统一立场；站在对面或脱离群体的人，容易呈现分歧。

Grouping

The geographic organization of characters within a scene establishes their relationship to one another. Characters standing in a group or on the same side of the frame represent a unified viewpoint of the scene. Characters on opposing sides of the frame or isolated from the group relay a lack of unity or an opposing perspective.

© Film Reframed · Composition for Editing · p. 6

07 · 客观与主观：让观众怎么感受07 · Objective & Subjective Shots

你想让观众知道全局，还是跟着一个人一起受惊？Should viewers know the whole situation or be surprised with one character?

我对此有什么感觉？

重要的不只是看见什么，还包括怎样理解它；看见谁的视角，会改变我们对情境的想法和感觉。构图前先决定要客观呈现，还是贴近某个人物。讲义用客观画面建立逻辑、主观画面建立情绪来强调区别，但不是说两者不能兼有。

How Do I Feel About It?

It’s not only what’s seen that matters, but how it’s interpreted. How an image will be interpreted will depend on which perspective you see. Which perspective we see is essential to how we think and feel about the situation! When creating a shot, we first need to choose if we’re going to shoot it objectively or subjectively.

客观与主观

客观镜头：不特别表达某个人物的视角。主观镜头：跟随一个人物的感受或观看角度，不一定只指字面上的第一人称镜头。

Objective vs Subjective Shots

Objective: a shot where no particular character’s perspective is being expressed. Subjective: a shot where we follow a character’s perspective. We need objective images to create logic. We need subjective images to build emotion.

走廊相遇：两种设计

例子：A 穿过走廊，在转角撞见 B。主观拍法可以始终跟着 A，使观众与她一起被吓到；客观拍法可以同时交代 A 和另一侧的 B，让观众提前知道即将碰面，形成悬念或期待，也可能担心她出丑。

Let’s Say a Screenplay Says

Character A walks through a hallway and rounds the corner, running into character B. Shooting subjectively might look like: a) We stay with A the entire time... and are startled with her. Shooting objectively might look like: b) We’re omniscient. We see A walking down the hallway and B coming down the other side. This could create suspense (“Oh no, what’s going to happen?!) or anticipation (“Oh no! They are going to run into each other! She’s going to embarrass herself!”)

© Film Reframed · Composition for Editing · p. 7

08 · 同样是主观，站在谁那一边？08 · Subjective Versus Subjective

让观众同情谁，要在机位和构图中作选择。Choose whose perspective will guide empathy.

同样是主观，站在谁那边？

拍摄两个敌对人物的冲突时，主观视角属于谁非常重要。仰看一个高大、居高临下的人，可能让观众替弱势者担心；俯看一个小而紧张、向上望的人，则可能使观众感到不耐烦或自信，认为对方过于挑衅。

Subjective vs Subjective Shots

When shooting subjectively, whose perspective we’re seeing is important! Let’s say we’re filming a confrontation, two characters at odds with each other. We could: a) See a big, towering man looking down at us. We’ll probably get nervous for the underdog. (“Oh no, he’s going to get slaughtered! I don’t want him to get hurt!”) On the other hand, we could: b) See a small, nervous man looking up at us. Maybe then we feel annoyed or confident! (“That little pipsqueak is so arrogant! He’s going to have to deal with the consequences of picking a fight!”)

导演的四个检查问题

检查四件事：这一镜主要传达信息还是感觉、逻辑还是情绪、情节还是主题？此场景要观众共情哪一个人物？谁的视角最有利于铺垫下一场？构图怎样支持这个意图？

So Ask Yourself

- Does this shot need to convey information or feeling/logic or emotion/plot or theme? - Which character do you want your audience to empathize with in this scene? - Which character’s perspective will set up the next scene best? - How do the compositions support that intention?

© Film Reframed · Composition for Editing · p. 8

11 · 练习 2：先用手机拍一遍11 · Exercise 2: Shoot a Scene on Your Phone

在正式开拍前，先验证这些镜头真的能剪起来。Test whether the shots cut together before the shoot.

和演员走位时，用手机拍下计划中的镜头，把素材交给剪辑师试剪，让对方指出衔接问题，以减少补拍风险。 动作戏、亲密戏、歌舞等复杂段落，需要在制作日程中留出调整走位的时间。若已有演员排练，更应趁排练时拍下并提前试剪。配图为讲义提供的十二格参考序列，保持原始阅读顺序。

2. shoot a scene on your phone When blocking a scene with your actors, shoot video of your intended shots on your phone. Send this material to your editor. They can try cutting the scene together and tell you if there are any errors. This can save you the hassle of potential reshoots! For complex sequences (e.g., action scenes, sex scenes, musical numbers) be sure to account for blocking adjustments in your production schedule. Even better, if you hold rehearsals with your cast, shoot it then and practice cutting it ahead of time!

© Film Reframed · Composition for Editing · p. 11

12 · 练习 3：重拍一个场景12 · Exercise 3: Recreate a Scene

不要复制机位：用自己的意图重新设计同一场戏。Redesign the scene around your intention rather than copy its angles.

重拍一个场景

选择一部喜欢的电影中的场景，或采用《早餐俱乐部》《十一罗汉》的场景，重新写分镜表或画故事板。

3. Recreate a Scene

Select a scene from one of your favorite films (or use the scenes from The Breakfast Club or Ocean’s Eleven), then use the following prompts to create a shotlist and/or storyboards of how you would shoot this scene.

A · 改善原场景

a）哪里可以改善？能发现原拍摄可能遇到的问题吗？哪里还能更有趣？

A) Is There Anything You Think Could Be Improved? Can You Identify Any Problems They May

have run into? Is there an opportunity to make it more interesting?

B · 个人风格

b）如果用你的个人风格，会是什么样？

B) What Does It Look Like in Your Signature Style?

C · 运动与走位

c）你会增加运动镜头还是固定镜头？人物会在空间里如何移动？

C) Would the Scene Have More Moving Shots? or More Static Shots? Would You Move The

characters around throughout the space?

D · 镜头数量与特殊角度

d）你会更省镜头吗？增加还是减少特写？会不会加入侧面、鱼眼或倾斜等特殊镜头？

D) Would You Shoot It More Economically? Would You Shoot More Closeups? or Fewer? Would

you add more specialty shots (profile, fisheye, dutch angle)?

E · 探索空间

e）是否探索空间：陈设静物、环境气氛和房间几何？

E) Would You Explore the Space (Still Lifes of the Decor, Atmosphere of the Space or The

geometry of the room)?

F · 细节与人物

f）是否强调被说到的细节，如《十一罗汉》的蓝图、对应对白形容词的画面、嘴唇或耳朵的极近特写？或者强调人物本身，如服装、姿态、听到信息后的反应、抖动的膝盖？

F) Would You Focus on Details About What’s Being Said (Coverage of the Blueprints in Ocean’s

Eleven, images that emphasize adjectives in the dialogue or an ECU of someone’s lips or ears)? Would you focus on who the characters are (details about their styling or posture, reactions to the information or a bouncing knee)?

H · 改变视觉锚点

h）某一刻改拍另一个人物会怎样？换掉场景的视觉锚点，又会发生什么？原讲义编号从 f 跳到 h，此处保留。

H) Would You Focus on a Different Character at a Particular Moment? What Happens If You

make a different character the anchor or point of focus?
